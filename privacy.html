3. Hosting and server logs

This website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, and details of your browser and operating system. This data is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure and stable.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of this website. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. GitHub's own privacy statement has further details.

4. Contacting us

Enquiries sent to the email address or phone number on the home page are received by Nada Consulting, Munich, which develops and operates Ground0 on our behalf.

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the details you give us in order to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry concerns a contract, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. We delete the enquiry once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention periods apply.

5. The assistants themselves

This policy covers this website only. Use of an assistant on its own subdomain of ground0.io is subject to separate privacy information, which is available there.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), and the right to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21). To exercise them, write to info@ahgmbh.com.

7. Right to complain

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
