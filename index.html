What the assistant does for your team.

These are things our own assistant does every day. Yours is connected to the tools your company uses.

  • Finds the conversationLooks up what was agreed with a customer across chat and message history.
  • Keeps track of invoicesLists and summarises open invoices, and prepares new invoices and order confirmations.
  • Keeps the CRM up to dateCreates and completes deals, so nothing depends on someone remembering to type it in.
  • Summarises the inboxTells each person what needs their attention in their email.
  • Reads documentsTakes in PDFs, including scanned ones, and answers questions about them.
  • Speaks your languageAnswers in the language your colleagues write in.

We currently connect to HubSpot, Lexoffice, Gmail and WhatsApp-based customer messaging. Other tools are added per company.
