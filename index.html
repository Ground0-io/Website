How it is built

Built the careful way.

An assistant that can act on your behalf has to be trustworthy first.

You stay in control

Before anything is created, changed or sent, the assistant shows what it is about to do and waits for your Confirm.

Each company separate

Every company has its own assistant, its own address and its own database. No data is shared between companies.
